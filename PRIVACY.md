# Privacy Policy

Journal Multilingual is an open-source Android application. 

### Data Collection and Storage
- **No Cloud Sync:** All data, including your journal entries and app content, is stored securely and exclusively on your local device.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register an account or provide personal information to use the app.
- **No Tracking:** We do not collect, share, or transmit your personal data to any remote servers.

### Third-Party Services
The app does not use third-party analytics, ad networks, or tracking tools. 

### Contact
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue in our GitHub repository.
